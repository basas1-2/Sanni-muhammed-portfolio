Get to know me

Hello! I'm Sanni Muhammed Arafat, a Computer Science student at Yaba College of Technology (YABATECH) and a Full-Stack Software Developer based in Ogun State, Nigeria.

My journey into software development began with a curiosity about how technology can simplify everyday processes. Since then, I have built applications for businesses and educational institutions, focusing on creating software that is practical, secure, and user-friendly.
